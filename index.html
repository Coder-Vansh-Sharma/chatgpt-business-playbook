Real talk about the $1 billion

A billion-dollar company is a long game: first ₹1 lakh, then ₹10 lakh, then a team and many products. No guide can promise it. This playbook gives you the exact first steps, and those steps are the same ones every big business starts with.

Why digital product?

You make it once. You can sell it 1,000 times. No stock, no delivery, no shop rent. Good for a beginner.

Before you start

You need: a phone or laptop, a ChatGPT account, an email, a bank account, and ₹5,000 to ₹15,000 for testing. If you are under 18, ask a parent to open the ad and payment accounts.

The 3 levers

Profit grows in only 3 ways: (1) cheaper clicks, (2) more visitors who buy, (3) more money per buyer. Every chapter works on one of these.

Prompt 1: Find 10 product ideas
Act as a product researcher for the Indian market. My audience: [students / freelancers / shop owners]. Give 10 digital product ideas they would pay for. For each idea give: 1) the problem it solves 2) who exactly buys 3) price in rupees 4) how easy to make with AI (1-5) 5) one reason people would say yes. Then show the best 3 in a table.
Prompt 2: Check competition
I want to sell: [your idea]. List what similar products exist in India and worldwide, their usual price, what customers complain about, and 3 ways I can make mine better and different. Keep it simple.

Pick the winner

Score each idea 1 to 5 on: people ask for it, I can make it in 3 days, I can explain it in one line. Highest score wins.

4 filters for a winning niche

  • Painful: people want it fixed now (exam, job, money, time)
  • Paying: others already sell similar things
  • Reachable: people ask ChatGPT about it
  • Fast result: buyer gets a win in 7 days

Where to find real demand

  • Reddit, Quora and YouTube comments: what do people ask again and again?
  • Top-selling items on Superprofile and Gumroad pages
  • Instagram reels with many comments like "send link"
  • Ask 10 people on WhatsApp what they struggle with
Prompt 22: Mine pain points
I will paste 30 comments or questions from my target audience. Group them into the top 7 problems. For each: the exact words people use, how strong the pain is (1-10), and one digital product that could solve it. [paste comments]
Prompt 23: Customer interview questions
Write 10 short questions I can ask [audience] on WhatsApp to learn what they struggle with and what they would pay for. Do not sell. Questions must be easy to answer in one line.
Prompt 24: Pre-sell test
Help me test demand before I build. Write a short post and a WhatsApp message offering [product idea] at an early-bird price of ₹[price], with a note that it ships in 7 days. Also tell me how many yes replies mean I should build it.

Proof of demand

10 people say "yes, I want it" or 3 people pay early. That is a green light. No yes = change the idea, not the ad.

Chapter 2

Make the product with AI

Make a small, useful version first. Finish in 3 days. Not perfect, useful.

  1. Write the outline with ChatGPT or Claude.
  2. Write each part, one chapter per message.
  3. Edit it in your own voice. Add your own examples.
  4. Design it free in Canva. Export as PDF.
  5. Open the PDF on your phone. Fix anything hard to read.
Prompt 3: Outline of the product
Create a detailed outline for a digital product: [product name] for [audience] who want [result]. Format: [PDF guide / prompt pack / template]. Length: about 25 pages. Give chapter titles, what each chapter teaches, and one action the reader does at the end of each chapter. Use very simple English.
Prompt 4: Write one chapter
Write chapter [number]: [title] from the outline above. Use simple words, short sentences, a real Indian example, a step-by-step list, and a 3-point summary at the end. About 500 words.
Prompt 5: Make a 100-prompt pack
Create 100 ready-to-use AI prompts for [audience] to [goal]. Group them in 10 categories. Each prompt must have [brackets] where the user fills their details. Add a one-line "when to use" under each.
Prompt 6: Quality check
Review this product like a strict customer. Tell me what is confusing, what is missing, what is too long, and what a buyer would ask for a refund about. Then give a fixed version. [paste your text]

Do not

Do not copy other people's content. Do not promise income, jobs, or medical results. Always add your own value.

Build an offer, not just a file

Main product: solves the big problem.
Bonus 1: a quick-start checklist.
Bonus 2: ready templates or prompts.
Bonus 3: a short video or audio walkthrough.
Safety: a clear refund or support promise.

Buyers pay for the result and feel safe. Three useful bonuses beat 100 extra pages.

Chapter 3

Free store and landing page

Sell where?Use
In India (UPI, Indian cards)Superprofile
Outside India (dollars)Gumroad

Both let you start free. They keep a small fee per sale, so check the current fees on their sites before you price.

Set up in 6 steps

  1. Sign up and complete KYC. Keep PAN and bank details ready.
  2. Click "Add product". Upload your PDF or file.
  3. Add a cover image, product name, and price.
  4. Paste your sales copy (Chapter 4).
  5. Turn on payment. Buy your own product once to test.
  6. Copy the page link. This link goes in your ad.

What your page needs

  • Headline with the result
  • 3 to 5 benefit points
  • What is inside the product
  • Who it is for
  • Price and one big button
  • Questions and answers
  • Your support contact

Build trust fast (you have no fame yet)

  • Real preview pages or sample screenshots
  • Your name, photo and short story: why you made this
  • Refund or support promise in plain words
  • First 5 buyers: ask for a short review and add it
  • Contact: WhatsApp or email shown clearly

One page, one goal

No menu, no side links. The page has one button: Buy. More links means fewer sales.

Superprofile vs Gumroad quick notes

Superprofile: built for India, UPI payments, WhatsApp-friendly links.
Gumroad: global cards, easy for dollar pricing, simple digital downloads.
Check each site for current fees, payouts, taxes and KYC before you start.

Prompt 28: Handle objections
List the 10 doubts a buyer of [product] will have (price, trust, "will it work for me", time, quality). Write a short honest answer for each to use as FAQs on my page.
Prompt 29: Thank-you message and email

Test it like a buyer

Open your link on a phone, on mobile data, and pay once. If it takes more than 3 taps to buy, simplify it.

Chapter 5

Budget plan

Never spend money you cannot lose. Test small first.

₹10,000

A good starting test budget for 30 days.

ItemAmount
Product tools (Canva, AI)₹0–₹1,500
Test ads (14 days)₹6,000
Image and video tools₹1,000–₹2,000
Safety money₹1,500

The maths of ₹1 lakh

Price ₹299. Sales needed for ₹1,00,000 = 335 sales.
If 2 of 100 visitors buy, you need about 16,750 visitors.
If a click costs ₹10, ads cost about ₹1,67,500. Too much!

So: improve the page and the price first. At 5 buyers per 100 visitors the same goal costs about ₹67,000 in ads. A better page saves a lot of money.

Prompt 11: Make my budget
I sell [product] at ₹[price]. My test budget is ₹[amount] for 14 days. Make a plan: daily ad budget, expected clicks at ₹[cost per click], expected sales at 1%, 2% and 4% conversion, and the break-even point. Show it in a table with simple words.
  • Stop rule: if you spend 2x your product price with zero sales, change the page or ad
  • Scale rule: if you earn more than you spend for 3 days, raise budget by 20%

Price ladder: earn more per customer

StepExample
Entry₹99 prompt pack
Order bump+₹49 extra templates at checkout
Main₹499 full guide
Upsell₹1,999 course or group

Break-even formula

Max you can pay per customer (CAC) = price minus platform fee. If you sell at ₹299 and fees are about ₹30, your CAC must stay under about ₹269 just to break even. Aim much lower.

Prompt 34: Price ladder
Design a 4-step price ladder for [product] for Indian buyers: entry, bump, main, upsell. Give the price, what each contains, and why a buyer would say yes. Also calculate average order value if 20% take the bump and 5% take the upsell.
Prompt 35: Profit calculator
My numbers: price ₹[x], platform fee [x]%, cost per click ₹[x], conversion rate [x]%. Calculate cost per sale, profit per sale, break-even conversion rate, and how many sales I need for ₹1,00,000 profit. Show a table.
  • Test only one price change at a time
  • Track in a simple sheet: date, spend, clicks, sales, profit
  • Keep profit money separate from ad money

Video creatives and scripts

The 4-part script (20 seconds)

  1. Hook (0-3 sec): stop the scroll with the problem.
  2. Problem (3-8 sec): show the pain.
  3. Solution (8-16 sec): show your product.
  4. Call to action (16-20 sec): "Link below. Check it now."
Prompt 14: Video script
Write 5 video ad scripts of 20 seconds for [product] for [audience]. Use this structure: Hook (3s), Problem (5s), Solution (8s), Call to action (4s). For each give: spoken words, on-screen text, and the scene to show. Simple English, honest claims, no income promises.

Simple video workflow

  1. Pick one hook from Prompt 16.
  2. Write the script with Prompt 14.
  3. Record screen or generate scenes with Prompt 15.
  4. Make a voiceover (your own voice sounds most real).
  5. Edit in CapCut: cut dead air, add big captions, add music.
  6. Export vertical 9:16. Watch it on mute. Does it still make sense?

Video mistakes

Long intros, tiny text, no captions, fake-looking faces, and promising results you cannot prove. Clear and honest beats fancy.

Chapter 8

Set up and launch ads

Check first

ChatGPT Ads is new. Access, countries, formats and prices change. Always check the official page and the in-app rules before you pay.

  1. Go to the official ChatGPT Ads page: ads.openai.com.
  2. Create the advertiser account with your business details.
  3. Add billing and a payment method.
  4. Create a campaign. Choose the goal: website visits or sales.
  5. Create an ad group. Add the topics where your ad should show (context hints).
  6. Upload image or video. Add headline and text.
  7. Paste your Superprofile or Gumroad link.
  8. Set a small daily budget. Submit for review.
Prompt 17: Topics for targeting
My product is [product] for [audience]. List 25 questions people would ask ChatGPT right before they need my product. Group them into 5 topics. Use real, natural wording.
Prompt 18: Policy check
Check this ad and landing page for risky claims, fake promises, or anything that may break advertising rules. Mark each risk and suggest safer words. [paste ad and page text]
  • Test 3 ads, 1 budget
  • Run at least 3 days before judging
  • Never change everything at once

Chapter 10

Your 30-day plan

  1. Days 1-3: Pick idea. Run Prompts 1 and 2.
  2. Days 4-8: Build product. Run Prompts 3 to 6.
  3. Days 9-11: Set up Superprofile or Gumroad. Test-buy it.
  4. Days 12-14: Write copy. Make images and 3 videos.
  5. Days 15-28: Run ads. Check every day. Fix one thing at a time.
  6. Days 29-30: Review results. Keep winners. Plan product 2.

Golden rules

  • Be honest in every ad
  • Give real value
  • Reply to customers fast
  • Test small, then scale
  • Never stop learning
